Product-level synergies
Transport
Closes the itinerary gap
Greytt builds day-by-day plans but has no bookable transport layer. Airport transfers, intercity routes, and private cars are natural day-1 items — SuperGryd fills this gap immediately, making itineraries actionable rather than advisory.
eSIM
Solves a 50+ pain point
International connectivity anxiety is disproportionately high in the 50+ segment. An eSIM offer at itinerary creation — before the trip — is high-relevance, low-friction, and directly addresses the "confidence" pillar of Greytt's value proposition.
Booking ownership
Keeps users in-app
Unlike Agoda/Viator affiliates where users leave Greytt to complete the purchase, SuperGryd's API keeps the entire booking flow on-platform. Greytt owns the transaction data, the confirmation experience, and the post-booking relationship.
Phase 2 runway
Activities, Tours, Attractions
SuperGryd's full catalogue — currently scoped to transport and eSIM — covers activities, attractions, and tours across 130+ countries. A successful pilot creates a clear path to replacing Viator/GYG affiliate dependency with an owned, margined booking layer.

Expandable

Agoda is a live affiliate integration (zero cost, commission-based). SuperGryd is a proposed paid API partnership (₹3.75L upfront, net pricing). They are not mutually exclusive — Agoda covers hotels, SuperGryd covers transport + eSIM — but they represent fundamentally different business models.

Verdict
Best for Greytt's next stage of evolution — owning the booking flow, building margin, and accumulating user data. The right choice if volumes justify the investment and the team has dev capacity. Quick, zero-risk monetisation on hotels. The right choice as a parallel stream — not a substitute for a booking commerce layer. Already earning and should be maintained.
Key insight: SuperGryd and Agoda are complementary, not competitive. Agoda covers hotels (affiliate). SuperGryd covers transport + eSIM (owned booking flow). Running both in parallel is the optimal strategy — Agoda provides immediate hotel revenue with zero overhead; SuperGryd builds the commerce infrastructure for everything else.
